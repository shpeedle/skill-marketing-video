# Music and sound effects licensing

These clips are marketing, so every track must allow **commercial use**. Licence terms change: check the source's current terms when you download.

| Source                               | Cost        | Commercial use                     | Watch out for                                                            |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Freesound (freesound.org)            | Free        | CC0 or CC BY only                  | Filter by licence. Skip uploads that say they're ripped or not their own |
| Pixabay Music and sound effects      | Free        | Yes, no credit needed              | Some tracks get YouTube Content ID claims; keep the download record      |
| Free Music Archive                   | Free        | Per track: CC0 or CC BY only       | Filter by licence                                                        |
| Incompetech (Kevin MacLeod)          | Free        | Yes, with credit (CC BY)           | Heavily used, sounds familiar                                            |
| Uppbeat                              | Free / paid | Free tier needs a credit           | Download limits on free tier                                             |
| Artlist, Epidemic Sound, Soundstripe | ~$10–30/mo  | Yes, on **business** plans         | Personal plans don't cover brand marketing                               |
| TikTok Commercial Music Library      | Free        | Yes, inside TikTok                 | Business accounts can't use regular trending sounds                      |
| Meta Sound Collection                | Free        | Yes, inside Facebook and Instagram | Only for use on Meta's platforms                                         |
| `audio/sfx.sh` (ffmpeg)              | Free        | Yes, we generate it                | Simple tones only; no realistic recorded sounds                          |

Avoid the YouTube Audio Library: its licence covers YouTube videos, not other platforms.

## Creative Commons

| Licence               | Use here? | Why                                                                   |
| --------------------- | --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| CC0                   | Yes       | Public domain; credit optional                                        |
| CC BY                 | Yes       | Credit the author in the post caption                                 |
| CC BY-SA              | Avoid     | The whole video would have to be released under CC BY-SA              |
| CC BY-NC, CC BY-NC-SA | No        | Non-commercial only; a product promo is commercial                    |
| CC BY-ND, CC BY-NC-ND | No        | No adaptations; CC 4.0 counts syncing music to video as an adaptation |

CC BY credit, in the post caption: `Music: "Title" by Author (link), licensed under CC BY 4.0 (link)`.

## Before adding a track

1. The licence allows commercial use, with no NC or ND terms.
2. The uploader made it. Reject anything "taken from", "extracted from" or built from another app's sample packs unless that app's terms allow it.
3. Add a row to `audio/LICENSES.md`: file, title, author, licence, source link and the date you checked.
4. Note any credit the post needs.
5. Prefer instrumental tracks with a steady beat at 80–120 BPM, at least 20s long or cleanly loopable.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. For paid ads, a paid stock licence removes the doubt.
